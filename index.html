Rug shoppers want answers your page gives too late.

Your rug pages already hold most of what a buyer needs: dates, delivery, materials, reviews. On a phone those answers sit below the point where most shoppers stop. This audit shows where they leave, why, and seven changes that fix it on your furniture layout.

So what. The answers a rug buyer needs are on the page, but low down, and only the most determined shoppers dig for them: 84% of phone buyers scroll to the foot of a rug page, against 18% of phone non-buyers. The plan brings those answers up to where everyone already is: dates on the size buttons, The Details straight after the buy box, and the review count by the title.
